Time my love, has played its cosmic joke  
Bringing us together on the dreams of hope  
Leaving the souls today, standing alone and broke  
With our own wretched demons to cope

The season of youth masked the many flaws  
Pausing for seconds on tender moments and silent applause  
Yet today we stand, many questions unanswered, in claws  
Unsure, what got resolved, what torments, what was?

The fleeting minutes of our happy togetherness  
Quickly dissolved in the blur of what came after  
We stood in the shadows of a darkening future  
While far ahead, another lonely day waited for us

With no clarity what I am searching for in this twilight  
With no idea which fork in the path to take  
I walk silently, with no destination in sight  
Weaving dreams of hope every moment in darkness and light
