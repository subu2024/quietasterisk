### Dating rules

- Default: one chapter per Sunday, starting with Chapter 1 on Sunday, November 1, 2026. Through Chapter 7, Chapter N falls one week after Chapter N-1: Ch 2 Nov 8, Ch 3 Nov 15, Ch 4 Nov 22, Ch 5 Nov 29, Ch 6 Dec 6, Ch 7 Dec 13.
- **Holidays:** Thanksgiving is Thursday, Nov 26, 2026, so Ch 4 (Nov 22) is the Sunday before it and Ch 5 (Nov 29) the Sunday after. Ch 7 (Dec 13) falls in holiday-shopping season. Christmas is Friday, Dec 25.
- **Time skip 1:** Chapter 8 is Sunday, January 3, 2027, so Anika's December predictions can resolve. Two Sundays (Dec 20 and Dec 27) pass off the page; no chapter is set on them. Chapters 9 to 24 then run weekly from January 10 to April 25.
- **Time skip 2:** Chapter 47 is Sunday, October 31, 2027, the day before her job-stay prediction resolves (Monday, November 1, 2027).
- Season 4 and any chapter using market data take the Data Pack's as-of date as the scene date. Log it in the Ledger so later chapters stay consistent.
- **Buffer arithmetic (derived from canon):** $500 deposits start the day after the first payday (early November). Two deposits reach the $1,000 first target in early December, so Ch 8's default "buffer reached by mid-December" holds.
