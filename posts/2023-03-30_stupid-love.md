.. sometimes, love can be stupid .. seeing a silhouette of a person, the mind wanders and conjures up a complete love story .. it does not even pause for a reason .. 

> _As I walked past the door_  
> _Her pretty frame was the only one visible_  
> _in the darkness of the early morning_  
> _The light behind making her radiant to glow_
>
> - an excerpt from the poem [the flirt, aug 2010]

And this stupidity doesn’t stop with age .. it matures .. like this story of a old guy falling in love with a younger person and chasing her ..

> _Our eyes met for the first time at a wedding harbor_  
> _Me an old man and she many many years younger_  
> _Lust penetrated the gap as she blushed each time I walked past her_  
> _Under the dark night, I grabbed her, planting a kiss drawing her closer_
>
> - an excerpt from the poem [the old man and the girl, aug 2018]

the obsession can slowly take the path of deeper feelings like depression and loneliness .. a need remains unfulfilled while it seems the world around is satiated in awesome love and happy endings ..

> _A damsel called Loneliness_  
> _captivates me with her dark smile_
>
> - an excerpt from the poem [in love with loneliness, oct 1999](in-love-with-loneliness.html)

And we enter the realms of nature .. falling in love with the moon, the rain ..

> _Shining brightly in crystal white_  
> _she wrapped her arms around me_  
> _looking directly into my eyes_  
> _she shook her head albeit with a smile_  
> _I have never danced this way before_  
> _seeing you super-high, I feel wimpy-low_

> - an excerpt from the poem, [Strawberry Honey](strawberry-honey.html)

even the British royal wedding did not fail to inspire another of this inane writing

> _The cool droplets of water raining_  
> _Persuades the harsh sun to calm down finitely_  
> _Sensuously wrapping its arms around me_  
> _Shielding me from the solar glare sublimely_

> - an excerpt from the poem [Reflection, may 2011](reflection-in-the-rain.html)

Ah! the many forms of love .. yet nothing is real .. love is the feeling generated within you .. the other elements are merely a trigger ..
