# Acknowledgements

This book grew slowly. It took shape in conversations that didn't feel important at the time, in moments of uncertainty, and in long stretches of not having any idea what to do next. A lot of people contributed to this project without realizing they were doing so.

To the friends who listened without trying to fix my problems, and who let questions stay open just a little longer than was comfortable—thank you. To those who challenged my thinking gently (and occasionally, not so gently), I appreciate you refusing to let me settle for easy answers.

Thank you to the people whose stories appear here, directly or in the margins. Your willingness to speak honestly about the confusion, the disappointment, and the quiet resilience of everyday life made this book what it is.

I also want to extend a special thanks to artificial intelligence—specifically Google Gemini—for taking my prompts and somehow sketching the exact visual representations of my mildly chaotic mind.

Finally, thank you. If you are reading this, you are already doing exactly what this book asks: paying attention, even when there are no guarantees. That is no small act.
